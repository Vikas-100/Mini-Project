Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deepfake detection, model accuracy, and data security.

What is deepfake detection?

Deepfake detection is the forensic process of using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning models to inspect digital media for subtle computational signatures left behind by generative networks (GANs, Diffusion Models, and autoencoder face swaps). It analyzes pixel residuals, facial landmark geometry, eye reflections, and sensor noise.

How accurate is the DFD Model detection engine?

Our DFD Model ensemble architecture (combining Vision Transformers with EfficientNet-B7 and 2D-FFT spectral analyzers) achieves a verified benchmark accuracy of 98.4% across standard academic evaluation datasets such as FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF, and the Deepfake Detection Challenge (DFDC).

Which file formats are currently supported?

Currently, image verification is active for JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP formats up to 15MB. Video verification (MP4, AVI, MOV) and Audio verification (MP3, WAV) are currently in development and will launch in the next DFD Model update.

Can it detect both face swaps and full AI-generated portraits?

Yes. Our dual-classifier pipeline targets both boundary blending artifacts common in face-swap tools (such as DeepFaceLab and FaceFusion) and global frequency irregularities characteristic of text-to-image diffusion models like Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and Flux.

How does frequency domain (FFT) analysis spot deepfakes?

Generative convolutional layers frequently leave periodic high-frequency checkerboard patterns and spectral anomalies in the Fourier domain that are invisible to the naked human eye. Our FFT module transforms pixel values into frequency space to expose these synthetic mathematical signatures.

Is my uploaded media stored or shared?

No. We prioritize your privacy. Uploaded files are processed in an encrypted temporary sandbox exclusively for generating your forensic report and are automatically purged. We never store personal media, train models on user uploads, or share data with third parties.

Can I download an official verification certificate?

Yes. Every analysis generates a unique cryptographic Tracking ID (e.g., DFD-2026-90412) with a SHA-256 media fingerprint. You can view, print, or download this certificate directly from the AI Studio.
